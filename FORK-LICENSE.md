# Grant for fork-authored files

`NOTICE.md` inventories upstream and fork-authored paths and their licence terms. This grant covers fork-authored files only.

## Upstream files excluded

The 418 unchanged files under `lib/` and `include/`, plus root `COPYING`, `README`, and `RELEASE-NOTES`, come from `curl-8.22.0.tar.xz`. This grant does not cover them. `UPSTREAM.md` identifies the release; `UPSTREAM.sha256` records the imported digests. The upstream `COPYING` file supplies the `curl` terms (SHA-256 `82f2f4427d6545ee5aaac4f0b80428da6cc8ba41c2cf5da3a03680ec327b9681`); `NOTICE.md` gives the per-file licences and exceptions.

`lib/curlx/inet_ntop.c` and `lib/curlx/inet_pton.c` use ISC; `lib/vssh/vssh.c` uses `curl AND ISC`; `lib/md4.c` and `lib/md5.c` contain public-domain and BSD text. `lib/Makefile.in`, `include/Makefile.in`, and `include/curl/Makefile.in` carry the FSF Automake notice.

No fork-authored files are under `lib/` or `include/`. Root `COPYING`, `README`, and `RELEASE-NOTES` are upstream's; `README.md` is the fork's. No other upstream or third-party material is carried, including downloaded headers or external data.

## Fork grant

This grant covers all other fork-authored files: build and packaging, generated-configuration C, the Zig facade and translation header, runtime, tests, consumer, integrity scripts, and documentation. `NOTICE.md` lists them by path. The repository owner authorized licensing these additions under `curl`; this permission comes from the owner, not an upstream grant.

The grant does not change upstream terms or the ISC, public-domain, and FSF notices listed in `NOTICE.md`. No upstream author or other named party is claimed to have written, reviewed, endorsed, or be affiliated with these fork files. Shipping libcurl binaries or headers still requires the notice in upstream `COPYING`.
